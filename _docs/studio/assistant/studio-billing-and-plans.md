# Studio billing and plans

## Where to find billing

- **Subscription** — view current plan, compare tiers, upgrade, start trial, cancel.
- **Billing** — usage meters (projects, team seats, monthly tokens), payment history.

Workspace admins manage subscription and billing. Editors can use projects but typically cannot change plans.

## Plan limits

Plans include limits on:

- **Projects** — max projects per workspace
- **Team seats** — max members
- **Monthly tokens** — chat token quota across all projects
- **Actions per project** — max actions defined per project

When a limit is reached, Studio shows tips and blocks creation (new project, team invite, etc.) until you upgrade.

## Plans (typical tiers)

| Plan | Typical use |
|------|-------------|
| **Free** | Trial; limited projects, seats, tokens |
| **Starter** | Small teams shipping first assistant |
| **Pro** | Higher volume |
| **Business** | Large teams, highest limits |

Exact numbers are shown on the Subscription page and in plan cards.

## Usage meters

**Billing → Usage** shows:

- Projects used vs limit
- Team seats used vs limit
- Monthly tokens used vs limit

Token usage is summed from chat across all projects in the workspace for the current billing month.

## Checkout

Paid plans use PayPal subscription checkout from Studio. After payment, poll confirms activation. Yearly billing may show a discount badge on plan cards.

## Cancelling

Cancel at period end keeps access until the renewal date, then limits revert to the free tier. Reactivate before period end if you change your mind.
